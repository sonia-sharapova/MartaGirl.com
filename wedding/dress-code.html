Please avoid loud patterns and bright colors, and keep the color scheme below in mind when selecting your outfit.

Venue Considerations

The ceremony and reception take place outdoors. Most of the night we will be walking on hardwood flooring under the tent, but there will be moments when you must walk on grass, gravel, or mulch (you might want to avoid wearing stilettos or narrow heels). August weather at this time is typically 70–85°F. We suggest bringing a light layer for the evening.

The ceremony will be held outdoors with a rain backup tent and umbrellas available.
