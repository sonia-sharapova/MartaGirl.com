Can I bring a date/plus-one?

At this time we are not permitting plus-ones. There will be some exceptions to this as spots on the guestlist may open up as RSVPs trickle in.

Are children welcome?

No, this is an adults-only celebration.

What should I wear?

Please see the Dress Code section for details!

What's the weather like in August?

Typically 70–85°F with a possibility of evening rain.

Is the ceremony outdoors?

Yes, with a backup tent and umbrellas available in case of rain.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Yes, a golf cart is available and we will set up chairs with backs in our front two rows.

When should I RSVP by?

Early July 2026 (6 weeks before the wedding).

Can I take photos?

Please refrain from taking photos during the ceremony. We would prefer that phone use be limited throughout the event and that phones be put away when not in use.

Where should I park?

Blooming Hill Farm has parking available.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive between 3:45–4:00 PM.

How late does the party go?

The reception concludes at 10:00 PM. An afterparty will follow (details TBA).
